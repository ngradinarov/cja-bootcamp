Field Reference

What every field on a call record means. Each maps to a column in CALL_CENTER.csv, keyed to the member by GUID / DIGITALID / MRN prefix so call behavior lines up with their web, email, and survey activity.

Identity & Context

FieldMeaning
CALL_IDUnique identifier for this individual call / contact.
CALL_DATEDate and time the call took place.
DIRECTIONInbound (the member called us) or Outbound (we called them — e.g. a voicemail follow-up).
CHANNELHow they reached us: Voice, Chat, or IVR (the automated phone menu).
QUEUE / SKILLThe department / skill group the call routed to (e.g. Pharmacy, Claims & Billing).
AGENT_IDThe representative who handled the call. Blank when a call is abandoned before an agent connects.

Outcome

FieldMeaning
CALL_REASONWhy the member contacted us — their intent / topic.
DISPOSITIONHow the call ended: Resolved, Escalated, Transferred, Callback Scheduled, Abandoned, or Voicemail.
FCRFirst Contact Resolution — Y if fully solved on this one call with no follow-up, otherwise N. A core contact-center quality KPI.

Time Metrics (all in seconds)

FieldMeaning
WAIT (S)Time the member spent in the queue before an agent answered. This is the member’s wait — it is not counted in Handle time.
TALK (S)Time actively talking with the agent.
HOLD (S)Time the member was placed on hold mid-call.
WRAP (S)After-call work — time the agent spent finishing notes / tasks after hanging up.
TRANSFERSNumber of times the call was handed to another agent or department.
HANDLE (S)Average Handle Time (AHT) — computed, read-only = Talk + Hold + Wrap. The total agent effort per call.

Experience

🔬 Lab note: Sentiment and CSAT are intentionally left blank on auto-generated calls. On Day 4 of the training lab we’ll try to get a sense of these ourselves — deriving them from the call transcripts (e.g. running sentiment analysis on the raw dialogue) rather than having them pre-filled.
FieldMeaning
SENTIMENT Day 4The member’s overall emotional tone — Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
CSAT (1–5) Day 4Post-call customer-satisfaction survey score, 1 (worst) to 5 (best).

Content

FieldMeaning
TRANSCRIPTThe full multi-turn agent / member dialogue. Click 🔊 Narrate to hear it read aloud; it carries the raw emotional signal we’ll analyze on Day 4.
