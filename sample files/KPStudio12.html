Metric & Dimension Definitions

NameDefinitionData SourceTypeAdditional Notes
Member EnrollmentsTracks the total count of new member enrollments to evaluate the starting point of the New Member Welcome JourneyKPxP: PERSON,MEMBERSHIPMETRICOriginal data source Member DB. Batch data.
Email SendsThe total volume of emails deployed to the target audience via Marketo platform.KPxP: TARGET_HISTORYMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email DeliveriesThe count of emails successfully reaching the recipient's server without being rejected, used to gauge list reach.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email OpensThe number of confirmed times recipients opened the email, serving as a primary indicator of subject line effectiveness and initial engagement.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email BouncesThe aggregate count of emails rejected by recipient servers (sum of Hard and Soft bounces), indicating data hygiene issues or invalid addresses.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email ClicksThe number of recorded user interactions (clicks) on links within the email body, measuring content relevance.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email UnsubscribesThe count of recipients who explicitly opted out of future communications, used to monitor list retention and dissatisfaction.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is Marketo. Batch data.
Email Delivery RateThe percentage of sent emails that were successfully delivered, serving as a key indicator of sender reputation and list quality.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
Email Open RateThe ratio of opened emails to delivered emails, measuring the effectiveness of the subject line and send timing.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
Email Bounce RateThe percentage of total sent emails that failed to deliver, used to flag potential technical or data quality problems.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
Email Click RateThe ratio of clicks to delivered emails, providing a direct measure of how effectively the email content drives action.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
Email Unsubscribe RateThe percentage of recipients who opted out relative to the total delivered emails, used to assess negative engagement.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
SMS SendsThe total number of SMS messages dispatched to devices via the mPulse platform.KPxP: TARGET_HISTORYMETRICOriginal data source is mPulse. Batch data.
SMS DeliveriesThe count of SMS messages successfully transmitted to and received by the recipient's device.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is mPulse. Batch data.
SMS ClicksThe number of times users tapped on links included within the SMS message.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is mPulse. Batch data.
SMS UnsubscribesThe count of users who requested to opt-out of SMS notifications.KPxP: INTERACTIONSMETRICOriginal data source is mPulse. Batch data.
SMS Delivery RateThe percentage of sent SMS messages that were successfully delivered to the device.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
SMS Click RateThe ratio of clicks to delivered SMS messages, measuring the immediate engagement of the text campaign.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
SMS Unsubscribe RateThe rate at which users opt-out of SMS communications relative to successful deliveries.N/A: Calculated MetricMETRIC
